# Pre-Interview Drill

Run this 30 minutes before any interview. Not the night before, not in the morning. Right before, while your brain is warm.

## T-30 minutes

- [ ] Re-read `00-intro.md`. Say your intro out loud once. Time it. Cut if over 2 minutes.
- [ ] Re-read `01-career-narrative.md`. Refresh the arc and themes in your head.
- [ ] Open `02-star-stories.md`. Pick 5 stories whose tags match the role. Skim them.

## T-20 minutes

- [ ] Re-read the job description. Identify the top 3 things they care about.
- [ ] For each, decide which STAR story you'd tell if asked.
- [ ] Skim the company's recent engineering blog / announcements. One talking point.

## T-10 minutes

- [ ] Prepare 3 questions for them. Mix tactical (team structure, tech stack) and strategic (what's the team's biggest challenge in the next 6 months).
- [ ] Open your STAR stories tab. Keep it visible during the call as a safety net.
- [ ] Water. Bathroom. Headphones tested.

## T-2 minutes

- [ ] Stand up. Shake out your hands. Two slow breaths.
- [ ] Remind yourself: this is a conversation, not an exam. You're interviewing them too.

## During the interview

- **Tell me about yourself** → Hook → Arc → Now → Why here. Under 2 minutes.
- **Tell me about a time when X** → STAR. Result first if you're running long.
- **Why this company** → Specific to them. If your answer could apply to a competitor, rewrite it.
- **Do you have questions** → Always yes. Three minimum. Listen to the answer, follow up.

## After the interview

- [ ] Within 24 hours: send a thank-you note. Reference one specific thing from the conversation.
- [ ] Within 48 hours: write a retro. What worked? What caught you off guard? Which story didn't land?
- [ ] Update `02-star-stories.md` with any new framings that worked.
