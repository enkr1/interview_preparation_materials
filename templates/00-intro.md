# Self-Introduction

Your "tell me about yourself" answer. The first thing every interviewer hears. Keep it under 2 minutes spoken.

## Template

### Hook (1 sentence)

[I'm a ___ engineer who ___. e.g. "I'm a backend engineer who obsesses over making systems fail gracefully."]

### Arc (3-5 sentences)

[Connect the dots across your career. Why A to B to C? e.g. "I started at ___ where I built ___. That got me interested in ___, so I moved to ___ where I owned ___. The common thread is ___."]

### Now (1-2 sentences)

[What are you working on right now? e.g. "Currently at ___ building ___, focused on ___."]

### Why here (1-2 sentences)

[Adapt per company. e.g. "I noticed your team is solving ___, which connects to my experience with ___. I want to ___."]

## Tips

- Lead with what you care about, not where you graduated
- Numbers stick: "reduced latency by 40%" beats "improved performance"
- Practise out loud. If it sounds stiff, rewrite until it sounds like you talking to a friend
- Record yourself. Cut anything that makes you cringe
- The goal is not to recite. The goal is to remember who you are
